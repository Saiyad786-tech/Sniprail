Sniprail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short version: Sniprail never sends your screenshots, text, or any other data anywhere. Everything stays on your PC.

What Sniprail reads

  • Image files in your Windows Screenshots folder, the Xbox Game Bar Captures folder, and any folder you choose to add.
  • Images you copy to the clipboard, only if you turn on "Also catch copied images". Those are saved as files in Sniprail's own folder on your PC.

What Sniprail stores, and where

  • Text read from your screenshots so you can search them. This uses the text recognition built into Windows and runs entirely on your PC.
  • Your settings (bubble position, pinned images, extra folders).

All of this is kept in Sniprail's app data folder on your PC. Uninstalling Sniprail removes it. Your screenshots themselves stay where they are.

What Sniprail sends

Nothing. Sniprail has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not upload anything. The only online step is the purchase itself, which the Microsoft Store handles. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers that part.

When you choose to share or drag a screenshot into another app, that app receives it under its own privacy policy.

Children

Sniprail collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
